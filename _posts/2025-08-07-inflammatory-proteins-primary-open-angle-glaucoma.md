tags:

Our collaborative study, *Causal association between 91 circulating inflammatory proteins and primary open-angle glaucoma: a bidirectional Mendelian randomization study*, has been published in *Cytokine*. The study highlights FGF-19, IL-18, IL-18R1, and LIF as potential therapeutic targets and provides new insights into the prevention and management of primary open-angle glaucoma. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cyto.2025.157007).
