tags:

Our study in the *Journal of Translational Medicine* reports that fucoidan may alleviate angiotensin II-induced hypertensive retinopathy by modulating the SIRT1/NLRP3 pathway. The findings support further investigation of inflammation-targeted strategies for hypertensive retinal injury. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12967-024-04877-6).
