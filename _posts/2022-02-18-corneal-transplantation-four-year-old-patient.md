title: Corneal transplantation brings new hope to a four-year-old patient
tags:

Dr. Shuai Wang and the ophthalmology team at the Second Affiliated Hospital of Dalian Medical University successfully performed corneal transplantation for a four-year-old child with a corneal limbal dermoid. According to the hospital report, the child’s visual acuity improved from 0.1 before surgery to 0.4 on the first postoperative day.
