title: Systematic review evaluates secondary glaucoma after congenital cataract surgery
tags:

Our systematic review and meta-analysis has been published in the *American Journal of Ophthalmology*. Based on 36 studies involving 3,151 children and 4,717 eyes, the analysis estimated an overall incidence of 6.6% for secondary glaucoma following congenital cataract surgery. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajo.2024.04.021).
