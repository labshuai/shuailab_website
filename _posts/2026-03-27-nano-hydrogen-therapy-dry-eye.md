A research project led by Dr. Shuai Wang at the Second Affiliated Hospital of Dalian Medical University has been included in the 2025 Dalian Life and Health Guidance Program, with RMB 600,000 in project funding. Titled *“Nano-Hydrogen Therapy” for Dry Eye Disease: Mechanistic and Translational Research Through Modulation of Ocular-Surface Mitochondrial Dysfunction* (Project No. 2025ZDJH01PT092), the study will run from January 1, 2026, through December 31, 2027. It will investigate how nano-hydrogen therapy may modulate mitochondrial dysfunction at the ocular surface and assess its mechanistic and translational potential for dry eye disease. The project builds on emerging evidence linking mitochondrial dysfunction, oxidative stress, and inflammation to dry eye pathology; however, the clinical efficacy and optimal delivery of molecular hydrogen remain to be established.
