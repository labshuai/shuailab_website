New study identifies inflammatory proteins linked to primary open-angle glaucoma
August 07, 2025 August 03, 2026
glaucoma inflammation publication

Our collaborative study, Causal association between 91 circulating inflammatory proteins and primary open-angle glaucoma: a bidirectional Mendelian randomization study, has been published in Cytokine. The study highlights FGF-19, IL-18, IL-18R1, and LIF as potential therapeutic targets and provides new insights into the prevention and management of primary open-angle glaucoma. Read the paper.

Collaborative retinal imaging study published in IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement
January 01, 2025 August 03, 2026
retinal-imaging oct publication

We contributed to a multidisciplinary study that developed a high-speed swept-source OCT/OCTA/ORG platform for structural and functional imaging of the living mouse retina. The integrated system enables rapid, high-resolution visualization of retinal structure, vasculature, and light-evoked responses. Read the paper.

Systematic review evaluates secondary glaucoma after congenital cataract surgery
April 26, 2024 August 03, 2026
glaucoma congenital-cataract systematic-review

Our systematic review and meta-analysis has been published in the American Journal of Ophthalmology. Based on 36 studies involving 3,151 children and 4,717 eyes, the analysis estimated an overall incidence of 6.6% for secondary glaucoma following congenital cataract surgery. Read the paper.

New research explores a protective pathway in hypertensive retinopathy
February 15, 2024 August 03, 2026
hypertensive-retinopathy inflammation publication

Our study in the Journal of Translational Medicine reports that fucoidan may alleviate angiotensin II-induced hypertensive retinopathy by modulating the SIRT1/NLRP3 pathway. The findings support further investigation of inflammation-targeted strategies for hypertensive retinal injury. Read the paper.

2022

Corneal transplantation brings new hope to a four-year-old patient
February 18, 2022 August 03, 2026
corneal-transplantation clinical-care

Dr. Shuai Wang and the ophthalmology team at the Second Affiliated Hospital of Dalian Medical University successfully performed corneal transplantation for a four-year-old child with a corneal limbal dermoid. According to the hospital report, the child’s visual acuity improved from 0.1 before surgery to 0.4 on the first postoperative day. Read the hospital story.
